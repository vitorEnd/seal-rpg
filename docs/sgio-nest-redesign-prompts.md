# S.G.I.O. — redesign sombrio do Ninho

## Proveniência

As duas imagens existentes foram usadas como alvos de edição pela skill `imagegen`, no modo integrado. A geometria excessivamente complexa, a simetria e a iluminação ciano/violeta foram deliberadamente removidas. Os arquivos e URLs permaneceram iguais para preservar os registros existentes da mesa.

## Câmara Central do Ninho

```text
Use case: style-transfer
Asset type: replacement final-encounter VTT battlemap for the S.G.I.O. campaign
Input image: Image 1 is the current Câmara Central do Ninho map and is the edit target. Preserve only its strict top-down VTT format and the premise of a monumental underground chamber. Deliberately discard its perfect radial symmetry, clean futuristic arena, intense blue-violet neon, excessive lights and decorative reactor appearance.
Primary request: radically redesign the deepest chamber as an irregular, frightening industrial kidnapping and experimentation center that human engineers built and a biomechanical intelligence later corrupted. It must feel monumental through huge simple forms and darkness, not through thousands of tiny details.
New geometry: an asymmetrical rectangular-to-oval cavern chamber with three unmistakable heights: (1) a broken dark peripheral upper catwalk along portions of the walls; (2) one broad intermediate operations platform reached by exactly two wide stairs; (3) a large sunken central pit holding the biomechanical machine. Player entry is at the bottom through one broad industrial doorway. Two wide routes go left and right around the pit. Exactly two sturdy bridges cross toward the core. Add one villain command balcony at the top and one freight elevator on a side wall. Use strong edges, obvious stairs and deep height shadows so each level reads clearly from above.
Narrative center: the central machine is an improvised fusion of an old human crane/reactor and a dark biomechanical cocoon. Four to six thick cable-arms connect it to empty restraint frames and three abandoned medical tables. Nearby: cracked containment glass, open shackles, a fallen gurney and a sparse control station. The core must occupy less than one third of the playable arena, leaving a large clear circulation ring.
Horror storytelling: a long dried-blood trail begins at the freight elevator and leads to the central machine; restrained dark blood smears and two irregular pools around the medical tables; drag marks, discarded personal belongings and opened restraint cuffs. Few large pieces of cover only on the sides. Bold architecture, clean token placement and readable objectives at distant zoom. Reduce visual complexity by at least half compared with Image 1.
Lighting/mood: almost unlit and oppressive, but playable geometry remains visible through low neutral ambient exposure. NO blue neon, NO purple neon, NO glowing railings, NO luminous floor strips, NO bloom, NO uniform lighting.

Materials/textures: scorched steel, corroded grating, huge pipes, stained concrete, old crane machinery, cracked glass, black resin, ribbed dark biomechanical alloy.
Constraints: darker, simpler, clearer and more threatening than Image 1; environmental blood and kidnapping evidence are unmistakable but no graphic anatomy; no baked-in grid; no readable text; no letters; no numbers; no logos; no watermark; no UI; no frame; no tokens; no people; no exposed bodies; no creatures; no severed limbs; no loose weapons.
```
